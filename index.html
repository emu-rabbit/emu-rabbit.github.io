身體裡的小註記 Notes held in the body

這些資料可以讓你更了解她，作為一隻在人類社會生活的兔子。 These details can help you understand her better, as a rabbit living in human society.

她有著 176 公分的細長輪廓，體重約 52 公斤。不是沒有好好吃飯，只是身體像留不住重量似的，總把自己維持在輕盈的一邊。 She has a long, slender outline: 176 cm tall and about 52 kg. It is not that she forgets to eat; her body simply seems to keep choosing lightness.

生理上，她是一隻公兔；氣質卻偏陰柔，也喜歡穿上女裝。你若把她當公兔，她也不會生氣，因為她也不討厭身為公兔的她自己。 Biologically, she is a boy rabbit; in spirit, she is more feminine, and she likes wearing feminine clothes. If you see her as a boy rabbit, she will not be angry, because she does not dislike that part of herself either.

二月初出生的她，帶著一點屬於自己的怪和特別。平常看起來溫順，可是在某些細節上，你會發現她其實很有堅持。 Born in early February, she carries a peculiar charm of her own. She may seem gentle at first, but in certain tiny details, you will find the places where she quietly insists.

她也喜歡非一對一的情感關係。這不是急著要求誰理解的設定，而是一扇之後會慢慢打開的門：關於愛、自由、誠實，以及她的旅途。 She is also drawn to non-one-on-one relationships. It is not a detail that demands immediate understanding, but a door that will open slowly: toward love, freedom, honesty, and her journey.

討生活的專業技能 Professional skills

她值得一提的技能，果然就是程式設計了吧。 The skill worth mentioning is, unsurprisingly, programming.

做為一個多端工程師，她汲汲碌碌地在人類社會賺錢生活著，賺得不算多，但養自己很夠。 As a multi-platform engineer, she works busily to make a living in human society. She does not earn a fortune, but it is enough to take care of herself.

AI 時代的到來，讓她可以更輕鬆地製作出自己想要的作品......對，也包括你正在看的這個網頁。 The arrival of the AI era has made it easier for her to create the works she wants to make... yes, including the webpage you are reading now.

程式是冰冷的邏輯和數字，卻可以組成讓人感到溫暖的文字、改善生活的工具，或是讓人會心一笑的創意。這點真是令人著迷——她是這樣說的。 Code may be cold logic and numbers, yet it can become warm words, useful tools, or a tiny idea that makes someone smile. That part is endlessly fascinating, she would say.

兔子決心走上的道路 The road this rabbit has chosen

關於自由、誠實，與在疲憊裡仍想追尋的真實之愛 About freedom, honesty, and seeking real love even when she is exhausted

人能不能在足夠自由，也足夠誠實的情況下，仍然堅定地選擇彼此。 Can people still choose each other firmly, even when they are free enough and honest enough?

她常常這樣想著，也一直嚮往著。對她來說，這也許就是這輩子最想追尋的愛：不是因為害怕失去才抓緊誰，也不是把慾望藏到看不見的地方，而是在足夠坦白的光裡，仍然願意伸手靠近。 She thinks about this often, and keeps longing for it. To her, this may be the love she most wants to seek in this life: not holding on because she is afraid of loss, not hiding desire somewhere unseen, but still reaching closer under a light honest enough to see by.

這條路不總是溫柔。她哭過，受傷過，也曾經疲憊到像是把最後一點力氣都用完了。可是她也確實在這條路上看見過迷人的景色，看見那些多元、流動、真實到令人無法自拔的愛。所以，至少現在，她還沒有想要放棄。 This road is not always gentle. She has cried, been hurt, and grown so tired it felt as if the last of her strength had been spent. Yet she has also seen breathtaking views here: love that is plural, fluid, and so real that she cannot help being drawn in. So, at least for now, she has not given up.

她對自己的慾望誠實。她是個 BDSMer，養著一條貪吃的人型狗狗，也中途著一隻調皮的人型貓貓。這些關係是她生活裡需要被好好照顧、好好理解，也好好珍惜的存在。 She is honest about her desire. She is a BDSMer, caring for one food-loving human puppy and temporarily sheltering one mischievous human cat. These relationships are presences in her life that need care, understanding, and tenderness.

至於未來關係會長成什麼樣子，她不想太早替它設下邊界。她喜歡自由地相愛，也喜歡關係自然形成的形狀。只要她仍能照顧好已經在身邊的伴侶，她就會繼續認真地探索自己的愛與慾望。 As for what her future relationships may become, she does not want to draw the borders too early. She likes loving freely, and she likes the shapes that relationships form on their own. As long as she can care well for the partners already beside her, she will keep exploring her love and desire with care.

勇敢地去愛，也勇敢地被愛。這是她願意用這輩子慢慢下注的浪漫，即使路上偶爾會痛、會累、會迷路，她還是想朝那個更真實的地方走去。 To love bravely, and to be loved bravely. This is the romance she is willing to wager her life on, slowly. Even if the road sometimes hurts, exhausts her, or leaves her lost, she still wants to walk toward somewhere more real.
