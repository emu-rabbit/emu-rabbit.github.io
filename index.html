兔子的窗邊手記

窗邊住著一隻白色垂耳兔 A white lop rabbit lives by the window

害怕著寂寞，卻仍然勇敢地去愛。 Afraid of loneliness, yet still brave enough to love.

她渴望誠實，也渴望自由。 沿著屬於自己的兔生路途， 慢慢走向更柔軟、也更坦白的地方。 She longs for honesty and freedom, following her own rabbit path toward somewhere softer and more truthful.

如果你剛好來到窗前，她會向你微笑。 示意你再靠近一點， 透過這扇窗，認識她。 If you happen to arrive at the window, she will smile and invite you closer, so you can see her more clearly.

靠近窗前 Come closer 溫柔、自由、坦白地被看見。 Seen gently, freely, and honestly.
