身體裡的小註記 Notes held in the body

這些資料可以讓你更了解她，作為一隻在人類社會生活的兔子。 These details can help you understand her better, as a rabbit living in human society.

她有著 176 公分的細長輪廓，體重約 52 公斤。不是沒有好好吃飯，只是身體像留不住重量似的，總把自己維持在輕盈的一邊。 She has a long, slender outline: 176 cm tall and about 52 kg. It is not that she forgets to eat; her body simply seems to keep choosing lightness.

生理上，她是一隻公兔；氣質卻偏陰柔，也喜歡穿上女裝。你若把她當公兔，她也不會生氣，因為她也不討厭身為公兔的她自己。 Biologically, she is a boy rabbit; in spirit, she is more feminine, and she likes wearing feminine clothes. If you see her as a boy rabbit, she will not be angry, because she does not dislike that part of herself either.

二月初出生的她，帶著一點屬於自己的怪和特別。平常看起來溫順，可是在某些細節上，你會發現她其實很有堅持。 Born in early February, she carries a peculiar charm of her own. She may seem gentle at first, but in certain tiny details, you will find the places where she quietly insists.

她也喜歡非一對一的情感關係。這不是急著要求誰理解的設定，而是一扇之後會慢慢打開的門：關於愛、自由、誠實，以及她的旅途。 She is also drawn to non-one-on-one relationships. It is not a detail that demands immediate understanding, but a door that will open slowly: toward love, freedom, honesty, and her journey.

職業技能 Professional skills

她值得一提的技能，果然就是程式設計了吧。 The skill worth mentioning is, unsurprisingly, programming.

做為一個多端工程師，她汲汲碌碌地在人類社會賺錢生活著，賺得不算多，但養自己很夠。 As a multi-platform engineer, she works busily to make a living in human society. She does not earn a fortune, but it is enough to take care of herself.

AI 時代的到來，讓她可以更輕鬆地製作出自己想要的作品......對，也包括你正在看的這個網頁。 The arrival of the AI era has made it easier for her to create the works she wants to make... yes, including the webpage you are reading now.

程式是冰冷的邏輯和數字，卻可以組成讓人感到溫暖的文字、改善生活的工具，或是讓人會心一笑的創意。這點真是令人著迷——她是這樣說的。 Code may be cold logic and numbers, yet it can become warm words, useful tools, or a tiny idea that makes someone smile. That part is endlessly fascinating, she would say.

興趣 Interests
